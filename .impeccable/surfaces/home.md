# Public Home Surface

- **Mode:** persuade
- **Audience:** project lead preparing a handover.
- **Belief to earn:** context can be transferred with sources attached, uncertainty visible, and human approval in control.
- **Proof:** synthetic Project Chronos records and findings already present in RelayOps. Label them as a demo.
- **Primary action:** Open workspace → `/workspace`.
- **Must remain true:** no account is required; records are processed and saved locally in the browser; reconstruction is deterministic; approval is human-controlled.
- **Composition:** editorial lead-in, evidence-rich product composition, concise explanation of the lead-to-teammate handoff, final workspace action. Distinct sizes and roles rather than equal feature cards.
- **Avoid:** invented ROI, customers, quotes, integrations, generic stock imagery, gradients, decorative motion, and duplicated workflow navigation.
- **Accessibility:** visible focus, semantic navigation, useful image/diagram labels, responsive layout, reduced-motion support.
