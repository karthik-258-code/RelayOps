# RelayOps Visual System

## Approved direction

Evidence-led handover brief for project leads. Public pages speak with editorial confidence; working views stay compact and legible.

## Tokens

- Paper `#F7F8F5`; white `#FFFFFF`; graphite `#202725`.
- Secondary text `#64716D`; line `#E3E8E4`.
- RelayOps green `#246B59` and pale green `#E5F1EC` for progress and approval.
- Evidence amber `#89520F` and pale amber `#FBF0D9` for unresolved evidence.
- Conflict red `#9E4038` and pale red `#F8E9E6` for contradictions.
- Use an editorial display face only if locally available and bundled; otherwise retain a neutral system sans with clear scale and weight contrast. Use monospace for source IDs only.

## Composition and interaction

- Home uses asymmetry, generous paper space, a short product story, and one authentic synthetic-workspace composition as evidence. No invented social proof or outcome claims.
- Workspace navigation makes sequence, current stage, completion, and approval lock immediately legible. Stage bodies reflect each stage's actual job.
- Use restrained borders and offset soft shadows only when they clarify hierarchy. Avoid generic feature-card grids and ambient gradients.
- Keep all actions keyboard reachable with visible focus. Pair color with text. Support narrow screens and reduced motion.
- One authored entrance transition on home; workflow status changes are immediate and brief.
